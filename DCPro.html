The brief

You can't fix what you can't see

A dry-cleaning facility is a choreography of foot traffic and heavy equipment — presses, cleaning machines, conveyors, a busy front counter. When something bottlenecks or a zone runs too hot, it quietly drains time, energy and money. But it's hard to see a pattern by standing in the room.

DCPro turns the floor plan itself into the dashboard. It maps a facility's layout, then paints a heat map over it — revealing the high-traffic paths and equipment zones at a glance, so owners can pinpoint the real problem areas and act.
