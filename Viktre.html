The platform

Where the athlete is the whole world

Fans want closer. Athletes want control. Viktre gives both — one home built entirely around the athlete, connecting the private, professional side of their world with the public stage where fans live.

It's three products in one: a members-only network for athletes to connect and collaborate, an open network to engage fans and followers, and a nonstop stream of compelling content from every corner of an athlete's life.

My role

Built the app

I developed Viktre as a cross-platform Xamarin.Forms application — bringing the private network, public feed and live streaming together in one athlete-first experience.
