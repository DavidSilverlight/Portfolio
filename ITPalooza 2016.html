The brief

A whole conference, beautifully in hand

By its fifth year, ITPalooza had grown into a multi-day event — training days, a keynote, dozens of sessions, mini-hacks, sponsors and a sprawling venue. Attendees needed one place to make sense of it all.

The 2016 companion app pulls the feed, sessions, events, hands-on hacks and venue info together — so attendees can review and select sessions, leave feedback, and build the schedule of what they'll actually attend that day.

My role

Development · UI/UX

I built the ITPalooza 2016 companion app on Xamarin.Forms — a native, fully cross-platform experience, with a focus on polished interaction and visual detail.
