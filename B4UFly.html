B4UFly

Before a drone leaves the ground, one question matters: can I fly here? B4UFly, built for the FAA, gives unmanned-aircraft operators a clear answer — instantly checking whether airspace restrictions or requirements are in effect at a chosen flight location.

Airspace, decoded

The airspace, made readable

B4UFly translates the aeronautical chart every pilot has to reckon with into plain, at-a-glance guidance.

Class BBusiest airspace — authorization required.
Class CAround busier airports — check requirements.
Class DTowered airports — restrictions may apply.
Class E / GOften open for drone flight, with rules.
RestrictedProhibited or restricted — do not fly.
TFR / WildfireTemporary flight restrictions in effect.
