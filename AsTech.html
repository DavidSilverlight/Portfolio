The brief

Expert diagnostics, without leaving the bay

Modern vehicles are rolling computers — and a proper repair depends on accurate OEM scans and expert interpretation. asTech pioneered this with a patented remote-scanning device and a staff of master technicians who diagnose vehicles from afar.

The mobile app extends that service to wherever the work happens: shops view completed scan reports, submit new scan requests, and pull up the documents they need — keeping vehicles moving and cycle time down.
