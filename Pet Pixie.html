Pet Pixie · a social-sharing app for pet parents · released on iOS & Android

The idea

A little social world, just for pets

People love their pets like family — and they love showing them off. Pet Pixie gives every dog, cat and critter their own profile: a place for their best photos, their story, and a following of friends who adore them.

It's part social feed, part scrapbook, part care companion — pet parents share adorable moments, dress up the perfect profile picture, keep vet info handy, and never miss a reminder, all in one delightfully cute app.

My role

Built the app

I developed Pet Pixie as a cross-platform Xamarin.Forms app and helped ship it to both the App Store and Google Play.
