The brief

Lock down 28,000 tanks — without slowing the crew

A massive field rollout meant installation specialists had to fit a brand-new lock to tens of thousands of tanks, each with its own unique combination, and log every detail so the records stayed airtight. Doing that on paper — at that scale, on a tight deadline — was a non-starter.

The Nalco Lock App put the whole procedure in the installer's hand: scan the tank, configure the lock, generate a one-of-a-kind combination, and push a complete, verified record to the backend before moving to the next unit.

My role

Built & delivered under deadline

I developed the Nalco Lock App as a cross-platform Xamarin.Forms field tool and delivered it on a compressed timeline — the schedule the 28,000-tank installation depended on.
