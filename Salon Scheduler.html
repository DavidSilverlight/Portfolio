The brief

Run the chair, not the paperwork

A busy salon lives and dies by its book. Between walk-ins, regulars, reminders and a team of stylists sharing stations, keeping the day straight is a job of its own — and one no artist wants to spend their time on.

Salon Scheduler puts the whole operation on the Clover POS: quick, easy appointment booking, automatic SMS reminders, client notes, and a journal of past orders — built to handle unlimited employees and stations, all kept in sync.

My role

Built for Clover

I developed Salon Scheduler as a Clover POS application on Xamarin.Forms — bringing scheduling, reminders and multi-station sync together in one tool for salon professionals.
