No. 01 — The Brief
The brief

Health care, reduced to one tap

MVP Health Care serves hundreds of thousands of members across New York and Vermont. Gia is how those members carry their health plan with them: an app that answers the 3 a.m. question — "do I wait, call, or go in?" — with an actual clinician, around the clock.

From one home screen, members reach virtual care, search doctors and facilities, pull up ID cards, and understand their claims. The virtual side is tiered the way real life is: urgent care by phone for the sudden stuff, everyday care by in-app message for prescriptions and routine conditions, and behavioral health by video — therapy and medication management included.

And because an unread bill is its own health hazard, every claim opens into a plain-language breakdown — what was charged, what MVP covered, and what's actually yours to pay.

"Get care, get answers, get healthier — whenever and wherever you need it."

The promise the UI had to keep

* Virtual care through Gia is available 24/7 and is free for most MVP members.

No. 03 — The Money
Cost transparency

Even better — it saves you money

Claims in Gia read like a receipt, not a riddle. Each one shows the provider's charges, what MVP covered, and the member's actual cost — with "Explain my bill" and service details one tap deeper, and the formal Explanation of Benefits attached as a PDF.

  • Plain-language claim status — submitted, processed, paid
  • "MVP Saved You" line makes the plan's value visible on every claim
  • EOB and determination letters available right from the claim
