The Brief

Turn a punch-card loyalty program into a pocket companion that customers reach for every single day.

Holiday Oil runs one of Utah's best-known convenience-store chains — over 60 neighborhood stores where regulars stop daily for fuel, coffee, slushes and snacks. Their loyalty program lived on physical cards and in-store signage. They wanted it in their customers' pockets: enrollment, points, rewards, coupons, store finding and car-wash activation, all in one friendly app that matched the brand's playful personality (clown mascot included).

The experience had to work at gas-station speed. A member standing at the register — or sitting at the pump — needed their scannable barcode and point balance on screen within seconds of launch, with weekly specials and the member fuel discount front and center.

Onboarding got the same treatment as the brand itself: a lighthearted photo tour (starring some very good dogs) walks new members through what Holiday Rewards does before a single form field appears.

Chapter 03

The Loyalty Engine

Three reward systems, one wallet: a scannable points barcode, point-funded coupons, and punch-card style clubs — all synced live over REST.

🎫

Scan-to-Earn Barcode

The member's loyalty ID renders on-device as a register-ready barcode. Cashiers scan it straight off the home screen — points post to the account in real time.

Admit one · every visit counts
💵

Points → Coupons

A live points ledger converts effort into value: 400 points buys a $1 mobile coupon, scaling to $5. Balances, costs and redemptions all flow through the rewards API.

400 pts = $1 · 2,000 pts = $5
