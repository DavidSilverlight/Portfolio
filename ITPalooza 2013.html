The brief

A conference that fits in your hand

ITPalooza packs a lot into a day — dozens of sessions, speakers, exhibitors and a map to navigate it all, in the name of learning and giving back. Attendees needed a way to find what mattered to them and not miss it.

The companion app lets attendees review and select sessions, rate sessions and the conference, and build a personal schedule of exactly what they plan to attend that day — turning a packed program into a plan.

My role

Architecture · IxD · UI/UX

I handled the architecture, interaction design and UI/UX for the ITPalooza 2013 companion app, delivered cross-platform on Xamarin.
